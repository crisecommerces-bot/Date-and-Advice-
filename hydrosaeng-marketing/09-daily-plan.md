# 14-Day First-Sales Sprint

Goal of this sprint: remove the trust/compliance leaks, get the buy button where your
audience is (TikTok Shop), post daily in your two winning formats, and buy your first
real sales data with a tiny paid test. Do the ONE bold task each day first.

**One post/day, TikTok. Repurpose each to IG Reels. Reply to every comment for 30 min after posting.**

## What "working" looks like at Day 14
- Rising avg watch-time / a video breaking past ~1k views (hook is landing)
- Spark Ad CTR > ~1% and some add-to-carts (funnel is alive)
- Your first 1–5 sales (usually via TikTok Shop before the website)

If none of that moves after real reps, the problem is the **offer**, not the content —
and that's the honest signal to change the product or positioning, not post more.
