## UX / trust bugs (2-minute fixes)
- Serum variant labeled **"NET WT"** — it's actually the PDRN type (Salmon vs Rose). Relabel "Type".
- Mask variant labeled **"COLOR"** — it's actually pack size (1Box-4Pack / 2Box-8Pack). Relabel "Size".
- "4.9★" with no review count/text reads fake — add a real count + a couple of real
  reviews, or remove the star rating until you have them.

## Rule of thumb going forward
Describe how skin **looks** or **feels** ("visibly smoother," "looks more hydrated,"
"appears plumper," "feels calmer"). Never describe what the product **does** inside the
skin ("repairs," "rebuilds," "regenerates," "stimulates," "penetrates," "heals").
