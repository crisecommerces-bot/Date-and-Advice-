Format: testimonial + habit-narrative (your proven winners). All ~20–35s, 9:16.
Every line is appearance-/experience-based — **no mechanism or drug claims.**
Each script: spoken VO · visual beats · on-screen text · caption. Generate or film on your side.

Rule when you improvise: describe how skin **looks/feels** ("looks smoother," "feels
calmer," "appears plumper") — never what it **does** ("repairs," "rebuilds," "regenerates").
