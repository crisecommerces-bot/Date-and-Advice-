# Phase 1 — Root-Cause Diagnosis

Grounded only in the real site + real numbers. TikTok: 312 followers, 22 videos,
38–174 views, ~20 likes. IG: 2 followers. Sales: 0. Traffic: minimal.

## The context that reframes everything
"PDRN Pink Peptide Serum" is **Medicube's** flagship viral product, sold at Ulta,
Amazon, Walmart, YesStyle with millions in brand marketing behind it. HydroSaeng is
selling an own-labeled white-label version (AutoDS dropship) **named identically** to
a product people can already buy — faster, cheaper, more trusted — elsewhere.

## Diagnosis in priority order

### 1. Hook problem — pending real video audit
22 videos at 38–174 views = not throttled, **ignored in the first second**. Almost
always a first-frame + hook problem. Needs the 3 best / 3 worst TikToks (with hooks +
view counts) to audit precisely.

### 2. Content / format problem
The 14-day test already proved **testimonial + habit-narrative win**. Still posting six
pillars dilutes the two that work. Cut the other four.

### 3. Offer / product problem — TOP 2
White-label generic wearing Medicube's product name = lose-lose. Buyers who know the
name expect Medicube; buyers who don't have no reason to trust HydroSaeng. $29.99 is
no-man's-land: not cheap enough to beat Amazon, not premium enough to justify the risk
from an unknown brand.

### 4. Website / conversion problem — TOP 2
- **Hero image is a woman in sunglasses** — for a "glass skin" skincare brand, the one
  thing a cold visitor needs to see (skin) is hidden. Reads as eyewear, not skincare.
- **No believable social proof.** "4.9★" with no count/text; gallery is "editorial
  portraits" (models). A 2-follower brand asking for a card number has no trust.
- Copy is genuinely good and on-brand. Writing is NOT the problem — trust + hero are.

### 5. Channel problem — why 0 sales TODAY
0 sales is mostly a **traffic** problem, not a checkout problem. With minimal traffic,
even a perfect page yields ~0 sales. Pure cold organic from a standing start is the
hardest possible way to a first sale.

## THE VERDICT — the single biggest thing
> HydroSaeng is a zero-trust unknown selling a me-too copy of a product people already
> trust elsewhere, through the one channel (cold pure-organic) that sends almost no
> traffic. The few who arrive have no reason to choose it over the "real" thing, and
> there aren't enough of them to brute-force a sale. **Content and copy are not the
> primary problem — the product's reason-to-exist and the traffic source are.** Fix
> order: (1) believable reason-to-buy, (2) traffic source, (3) then hooks.
