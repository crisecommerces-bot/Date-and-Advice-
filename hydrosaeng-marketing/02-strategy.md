# Phase 2 — The Fix (Strategy)

Attacks the real problems (reason-to-buy + traffic), in order.

## 1. Positioning wedge (the real unlock)
You cannot beat Medicube using Medicube's product name and look. The only defensible
angle for a white-label is an audience Medicube treats as an afterthought:
**women 30s–40s who are done with harsh actives.** Your own copy already leans this way
("without the harshness of stronger actives, no stinging, no purge phase"). Lean in all
the way. **Sell the audience + the ritual, not the copycat product name.**

## 2. Content system (organic)
Kill the other four pillars. Post ONLY **testimonial + habit-narrative**.
- **TikTok:** 1/day, 6–7/week, 3–4 weeks straight. Batch with Sora. 21–34s, hook in
  first 1s, on-screen text, native look, trending-adjacent audio.
- **Instagram:** repurpose the TikToks (4–5/week) + 1–2 before/after carousels/week.
  Don't create net-new for IG.

## 3. Hook framework — "Call-out → Pain → Curiosity gap" (first 2s)
Name the person, name the pain, open a loop. Compliant examples for this product:
1. "If you're in your 30s and your skin looks 'tired' no matter how much you sleep — watch this."
2. "I quit retinol for 30 days and used this pink serum instead. Here's what my skin looks like now."
3. "POV: a serum that doesn't sting, doesn't peel, and still makes your skin look glassy."
4. "Nobody warns you how your skin changes at 35. This is the 3-step ritual I do now."
5. "My 5pm mirror check used to depress me. Three weeks in, I actually like what I see."

## 4. Organic vs paid — recommendation: add both
- **TikTok Shop + affiliate program (highest leverage).** Puts the buy button inside the
  app and lets TikTok carry the checkout trust an unknown domain can't. Recruit 5–10
  micro-creators to post testimonial UGC on commission. Attacks traffic AND trust.
- **Small Spark Ads test.** Boost your 1–2 best organic testimonials at $15–20/day for
  ~10 days (~$150–200). Goal = first-sale data: 100 targeted visitors → 0 sales means
  the offer/page is broken; 2–3 sales means a funnel to scale.

## 5. Highest-leverage first moves (in order)
1. **Today (free):** swap the sunglasses hero for a skin-forward shot; fix the compliance
   spots + 2 variant-label bugs (see 05); make "4.9★" real or remove it.
2. **This week:** open TikTok Shop, enroll affiliates, recruit 5–10 micro-creators.
3. **This week:** batch testimonial/habit-narrative videos with Sora; run the $150–200
   Spark Ads test on the best one.
