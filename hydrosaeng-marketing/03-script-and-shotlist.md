## Compliance checkpoint — borderline lines flagged for sign-off
1. **"I quit retinol… replaced it with this."** — personal choice, not an efficacy claim.
   Compliant, but implies parity with an active. Keep, or soften to "I gave my skin a
   break from retinol."
2. **"tight, flaky, never quite happy"** — describes prior skin look/feel (allowed).
   Deliberately avoided "irritated/damaged" (imply a condition). Clean.
