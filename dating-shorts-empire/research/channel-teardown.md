## Strategic conclusions for OUR channel
1. **Differentiate on format, not topic:** same proven topics, but an animated 2D mascot (Ace) + story-first voice = distinct on a grid full of talking heads.
2. **Voice:** friend-who's-been-through-it (stories + honesty), not guru. Contrarian hooks, never manipulative advice — this also protects the affiliate trust.
3. **Both-audience positioning:** Kyle is men-only; Hussey is women-only. Ace speaks to anyone dating — wider ceiling, and red/green flag framing is gender-neutral.
4. **Every Short ends before it feels done** — loop-friendly cuts; comments farmed with "what's YOUR red flag" prompts.
