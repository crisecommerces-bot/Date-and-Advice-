# Growth Playbook — First 1,000 Subscribers on Shorts

## The one metric that decides everything
**Swipe-away rate in the first 2 seconds** (Studio → Analytics → Engagement → "Viewed vs. swiped away"). 
Rule: a script FORMAT gets repeated only if ≥70% stayed past the hook. Below that, the format (not just the video) goes to the bench. Retention graph cliff at a specific beat = that beat style gets cut from future beat maps. This is the whole feedback loop — everything else is commentary.

## Posting time
- Daily, same slot: **6–8 PM local** (evening scroll). Consistency > perfect timing; the algorithm tests Shorts over 24–72h anyway.
- Never dump 3 videos at once; one per day builds the habit loop.

## Comment engine (comments are a ranking signal)
- Every description/pinned comment ends in a question that invites STORIES (already built into all 33 packages).
- **Hour one: reply to 100% of comments.** Replies double the comment count and tell the algorithm the video is alive.
- Pin a comment with the affiliate link + question. Heart good stories, pin the best story after 24h (rotates engagement).
- Reply format: 1 line, ask a follow-up ("how long did you stay after that?") — threads > likes.

## A/B the covers (01A vs 01B)
- Covers only affect grid + search, not the feed. Test on the intro: 01A ("100 DATES / 2 YEARS", confident) first 48h → swap to 01B ("I DATED 100 / SO YOU DON'T", talking pose) → keep the winner pattern for future covers.
- What you're reading: channel-page click-through in Studio (Content → grid impressions), not feed views.

## Community tab (unlocks early; use it from day 1 if available)
Three ready-to-post drafts:
1. **Poll:** "What's the bigger red flag on date one? 🚩 A) On their phone constantly B) Rude to staff C) Only talks about their ex D) 'I hate drama'" — post after video #2.
2. **Story bait:** "Finishing next week's video. In one sentence: the exact moment you KNEW you had to leave a date. Best one gets featured (anonymously)." — post after video #4.
3. **Book tease:** "The book is real. 100 dates, every lesson, zero sugarcoating. Comment ONE question you'd want answered in it and I'll answer the top 3 in a video." — post week 2.

## Cadence math to 1,000 subs (estimate, stated assumptions)
- Daily Shorts, niche average conversion ~1 sub per 300–600 Short views for a new advice channel (unverified heuristic — treat as planning number, not promise).
- 30 videos × 5–20K median views at daily cadence with proven topics → 250–2,000 subs in the first cycle. The ranked list front-loads the highest-proof topics precisely to pull the median up early.

## Weekly review ritual (15 min, Sundays)
1. Sort last 7 Shorts by swipe-away rate. 2. Kill the worst format, double the best (series rule in `series-hooks.md`). 3. Re-rank next week's calendar accordingly. 4. Check affiliate dashboard clicks vs. views — if a self-work-topic video overperforms on clicks, schedule its sibling idea (#16, #17, #18, #27, #30) next.
