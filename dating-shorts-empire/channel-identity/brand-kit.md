## Visual motif (committed)
**Red flag 🚩 / green flag ✅ pennants.** The mascot literally raises a small red or green pennant flag to score behaviors. Appears in covers, Shorts beats, and the banner. One motif, repeated forever = instant recognition on the Shorts grid.

## The mascot — "Ace" (user-directed redesign)
Simple, friendly, flat 2D vector host: athletic young man, thick messy wavy **black** hair, strong straight eyebrows, defined jawline, warm tan skin, plain **black crew-neck tee with a small green "A" chest logo** (A = Advice/Ace), dark navy pants, white sneakers. Bold Ink-Navy outlines, flat fills, cream background. Big expressive eyes/eyebrows (emotion is the product). Reads instantly at 98×98 px.
Expression set: neutral · smitten (heart-eyes) · suspicious side-eye · shocked · facepalm · confident smirk · waving.
Master reference: `channel-identity/master-character-sheet.png` (Higgsfield job `f83fab6f-3e37-4157-8af3-a459fcbb5bdc`), registered as reference element **`Ace-DatingMascot-Master`** (`1949b5d7-5025-4ff4-bd7b-e456848c1f58`) — **every** avatar generation (Shorts scenes and covers) embeds this element. v1 (hoodie concept) and v2 (pre-logo) sheets kept for provenance.
