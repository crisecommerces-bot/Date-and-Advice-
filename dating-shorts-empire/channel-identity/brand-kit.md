## Visual motif (committed)
**Red flag 🚩 / green flag ✅ pennants.** The mascot literally raises a small red or green pennant flag to score behaviors. Appears in covers, Shorts beats, and the banner. One motif, repeated forever = instant recognition on the Shorts grid.

## The mascot — "Wing"
Simple, friendly, flat 2D vector wingman: rounded shapes, bold Ink-Navy outlines, warm tan skin, short dark hair, coral-red hoodie with a tiny green wing emblem, cream background. Big expressive eyes + eyebrows (emotion is the product). Reads instantly at 98×98 px.
Expression set: neutral · smitten (heart-eyes) · suspicious side-eye · shocked · facepalm · confident arms-crossed · waving.
Master reference: `channel-identity/master-character-sheet.png` — **every** avatar generation (Shorts scenes and covers) references this sheet.
